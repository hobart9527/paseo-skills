## Why it is not met (measured, not inferred)

- The baseline's 53.8 min was ~60 % rework: three R0 rounds costing 9.9 + 8.8 + 8.3 =
  27.0 min, plus 5.4 min of gaogong revisions and a 4.3-min setup with three failed
  `create_agent` calls.
- The optimization removed that rework: R0 went 3 rounds → 1, blockers → 0.
- But the baseline-alignment work had to go somewhere. It moved into authoring:
  chanpin 11.1 → 14.0 min, gaogong 2.3 → 11.2 min (a 31-row baseline table plus two
  delta specs it previously omitted).
- Net: −16.6 min review, +12.8 min authoring = −11.0 min total.
- Arithmetic ceiling with this topology: authoring alone is 26.2 min, so **even a
  zero-cost R0 caps the reduction at 48.7 %** (27.6 min).

## Conclusion

The optimization works where it was aimed (review rounds and defects) but cannot reach
60–70 % on this workload: the irreducible authoring chain (two serial author agents)
already costs half the baseline. Reaching the bar needs a topology change, not more
prose.
